Practice essay prompts
Explain how British victory in the Seven Years' War led to the American Revolution.
  • Debt doubled, so Grenville taxed the colonies (Sugar Act, Stamp Act)
  • Proclamation of 1763 and Pontiac's Rebellion
  • Virtual vs. actual representation; taxation without consent
  • Boycotts, Sons of Liberty, Townshend Acts, Boston Massacre
  • Tea Act, Tea Party, Coercive Acts, First Continental Congress, Lexington and Concord
Compare the Federalists and the Republicans of the 1790s.
  • Leaders: Hamilton/Adams vs. Madison/Jefferson
  • Commerce and manufacturing vs. agriculture
  • Broad construction/implied powers vs. strict construction
  • Britain vs. France; Jay's Treaty and the French Revolution
  • Order and hierarchy vs. individualism and limited government
  • Alien and Sedition Acts vs. Virginia and Kentucky Resolutions
What were the causes and results of the War of 1812?
  • Neutral rights, ship seizures and impressment; failure of the Embargo
  • War Hawks led by Henry Clay; Canada and Florida; British aid to Indians
  • Tecumseh's death at the Thames; Jackson at Horseshoe Bend and New Orleans
  • Burning of Washington; Fort McHenry and the Star-Spangled Banner
  • Hartford Convention and the Federalist collapse; Treaty of Ghent left issues unresolved; new nationalism
